How it works

Four steps, two days, one field. Every selected player gets two games in front of the scouts before anyone advances.

  1. 1

    Register

    Players from any club, city, or state fill out the registration form. Boys and girls register on separate forms. There is no cost to register and no cost to play.

  2. 2

    Selection

    From more than 1,000 registrations, approximately 250 players are selected and placed into age groups by birth year.

  3. 3

    Showcase day November 3

    Each selected player plays two 30-minute games, one in the morning and one in the afternoon, with scouts from MLS and Liga MX academies watching from the sideline.

  4. 4

    The finals November 4

    The scouts pick 24 players from each age group to return for a final match. The finalists are coached directly by the scouts themselves. Goalkeepers get their own session: the GK War.

What we promise, and what we don’t

  • Scouts are on the field. Representatives from MLS and Liga MX academies watch the games directly.
  • Each club evaluates on its own. If a player stands out, the next step might be a conversation, a training invitation, a tryout, or another process. That is between the club and the family.
  • We do not promise contracts or academy spots. What we guarantee is a high-level environment seen by real talent ID professionals.
  • The event has no participation cost. Selected players purchase only the official $60 kit: two commemorative shirts they wear in the games and keep afterward.

Questions

Is United For The Game legit?

Yes. This is the third edition. Ten MLS and Liga MX academies have confirmed for November 2026, and players from past editions have gone on to academy rosters. Everything we announce is posted publicly on Instagram.

How much does it cost?

Nothing to register, nothing to be evaluated, nothing to play. If you are selected, you buy the official $60 kit, which is two commemorative shirts you wear in your games and keep. That purchase confirms your spot, and it is the only cost.

Who can register?

Boys born between 2006 and 2016, and girls born in 2012, 2013, or 2014. Any club, city, or state. You do not need to be on a specific team to apply. Boys and girls use separate registration forms.

Does registering mean I’m in?

Not yet. Registering puts your name in front of the organizers, who review every player and choose roughly 250 out of more than 1,000. If you are selected, you will hear from us directly.

Will I get signed if I play well?

We don’t promise that, and no one honest can. Each club makes its own evaluation and its own decision. What you get is two games, and possibly a final, in front of people whose job is to find players.
