Inside a professional academy

Players who were seen at United For The Game and are now training or signed with a professional club.

  • Ubaldo García

    Chivas de Guadalajara

    Known as “Tomate.” Ubaldo was selected to compete with the Chivas academy in the Copa Chivas, trained at the club in Guadalajara, and has since signed with Chivas. The account calls it the reason they do this.

    Read the signing post / Copa Chivas post

  • Jared Juárez

    FC Juárez

    From Las Cruces, New Mexico. After the last edition, Jared received invitations from Chivas, FC Juárez, Atlas, Club América, Atlético San Luis, and Pachuca. He now plays in the FC Juárez academy, and Bravos are coming back to Las Vegas in November to look again.

    Read the academy post / The six invitations

  • Four players

    Sporting Kansas City

    Three players from the United For The Game community, including Arnold Martinez, earned places in the Sporting KC Academy. A fourth came up through that academy and today plays professionally for the club. Others have traveled to Kansas City to be evaluated.

    Read the post

  • Henry Montoro

    Professional academy, Brazil

    A long-time player in coach Felipe Loyola’s development program, Henry joined a professional academy in Brazil in July 2026. His coach’s words: dreams are not achieved through talent alone.

    Read the post

  • Lucas Barragán

    Preseason with Chivas

    Lucas believed in the project from the first edition and played in its events. He was invited to take part in preseason with Chivas.
